Real-ESRGAN models
==================

realesrgan-x4plus, realesrgan-x4plus-anime, realesr-animevideov3 and
realesr-general-x4v3, downloaded from the official release of
xinntao/Real-ESRGAN (https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/releases/tag/v0.2.5.0).
realesr-general-x4v3 was converted from PyTorch to ncnn's format on this
computer; the others are upstream's own ncnn files. The repository publishes
them under its BSD-3-Clause license; no separate license for the weights is
stated (see docs/upscaling-ml-spike.md in CatConvert's source).

Wang, Xintao and Xie, Liangbin and Dong, Chao and Shan, Ying: "Real-ESRGAN:
Training Real-World Blind Super-Resolution with Pure Synthetic Data",
International Conference on Computer Vision Workshops (ICCVW), 2021.
